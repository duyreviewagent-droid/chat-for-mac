Chat

3D chat rooms for your Mac. Make your person, walk around, and talk.

Download for Mac

Free · macOS 14 or newer · Apple Silicon and Intel

🧍 Your personPick your name, skin, hair and outfit.
🛋️ 3D roomsCreate a room and pick its style: Lounge, Gaming, Music, Chill, Art or Sports.
🎲 Random roomJump into a room where people are hanging out.
🔑 Room codesMake a private room and send the code to friends.
💬 Speech bubblesMessages pop up over people's heads.
📜 Catch upJoin a room and see the last messages people sent.
💃 EmotesWave, dance and jump.

How to install

  1. Click Download for Mac and open Chat.zip to unzip it.
  2. Drag Chat into your Applications folder.
  3. The first time, Mac may say it can't check the app. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Chat. (On older Macs you can also right-click Chat and choose Open.)

Chats go over the internet, and anyone with a room's code can read that room. Phone numbers and emails are hidden automatically. Don't share personal info.
